# Spec D: Technical Decision Record

## Goal
Produce a one-page decision record covering two architecture decisions:
1. Centralized integration platform vs decentralized team-owned integrations
2. Event-driven vs synchronous request-response for critical flows

## Required Sections
- Context
- Options considered
- Decision
- Consequences

## Evaluation Dimensions
- Team autonomy vs standardization
- Change lead time
- Reliability and blast radius
- Operational complexity
- Governance and compliance

## Acceptance Criteria
- Decision rationale is explicit and balanced
- Tradeoffs and risks are documented
- Clear recommendation is stated for each decision

## Output Files
- `docs/assessment.md` (Section D)
